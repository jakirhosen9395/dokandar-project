# DOKANDAR Platform OpenAPI (generated)

`dkd-platform.openapi.json` is generated by `dkdgen` from the frozen contracts (`dkd-contracts-spine@1.0.0`) — **never hand-edit**; regenerate via `dkdgen generate`.

## Scope — what IS specified (from contracts)
- Universal operational endpoints `/health` `/ready` `/live` `/version` (implemented and verified in every runtime of the golden service template).
- Cross-cutting components mandated by the architecture: `ResponseEnvelope` {success,data,error,meta}, RFC-7807 `ProblemDetails` (taxonomy-pinned `code`), cursor `PageInfo`, bearer-JWT security scheme, `Idempotency-Key` + correlation headers, `Money` (int64 poisha), `TimestampMs` (int64).
- `x-dkd-ohs-services`: the 5 OHS services + operation names registered in canon (api-registry.yaml).

## Deferred by design — what is NOT specified, and why
Per-context business `/v1` REST paths and OHS gRPC `.proto` request/response signatures are **NEEDS-INFO** in the frozen contracts (`api-registry.yaml`: *"No endpoint invented"*, Phase-2). Emitting them would require fabricating specifications, which platform governance (P2) forbids. They are therefore **intentionally deferred**, recorded under `x-dkd-deferred`, and will be filled by a future additive contract revision — **not missing work**. See `docs/openapi-generation-and-deferral.md`.
